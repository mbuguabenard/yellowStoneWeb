# Image permission request templates

## Email

**Subject:** Permission request to feature your landscape project on our marketplace

Hello [Name],

We are building a Kenya-focused marketplace for landscape projects, plants, materials and professionals. May we have your written permission to feature these images from your project, **[project name / page links]**, on our commercial website and related marketplace pages?

We would credit you as **[preferred credit]**, link to your profile at **[profile URL]**, and identify the work as yours. Please let us know whether cropping or resizing is acceptable, any other conditions, and whether you control the rights to the images and any visible people or property.

If you agree, please reply confirming which named images we may use and the terms of permission. We will not use them unless we have your confirmation.

Thank you,  
[Your name]  
[Marketplace name]  
[Contact details]
